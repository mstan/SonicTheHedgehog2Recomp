Adds the optional **Clone Driver + Mega PCM** audio mod for local play.
Music, instruments, effects and samples are prepared from the player's verified
Sonic 2 ROM. The original driver remains the default; restart after changing
the selection. Disable the alternate driver and restart before netplay.

Current-build snapshots, rewind and diagnostic replay preserve the selected
driver. High refresh interpolation remains available under Presentation for
regular Sonic/Tails gameplay. See the included sound driver guide and notices.

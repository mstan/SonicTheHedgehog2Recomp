# Sonic the Hedgehog 2 0.9.7

This release addresses the player-feedback burndown: save-menu backgrounds,
fades, roster portraits, deletion sounds/cancellation, original Knuckles HUD
and item artwork, glide dust, spring poses, intro visibility, widescreen Sega
intro, Options and title cards. Both competition views now support widescreen,
independent cameras, the selected characters' HUDs and an opaque divider.

The launcher uses the current shared UI base with six-player controls and saves
audio and presentation preferences on close as well as Play. Optional practice
settings provide infinite lives and time. Optional 120 Hz, 144 Hz or display-refresh
presentation smooths regular Sonic/Tails play in native or widescreen aspect,
with unchanged game/input/audio cadence and one tick of visual latency.
Donor parties, transitions, special stages and split-screen retain ordinary
completed images. These enhancements are off by default.

The release retains the v0.9.6 engine optimizations, water-palette/menu-input
repairs, Windows rollback netplay and default-on replayable feedback. The build
uses precise native IPO and freshly trained normal PGO. Windows x64; supply
your own supported Sonic 2 ROM and optional character-art donor ROMs. No ROM or
extracted donor artwork is distributed. Preserve native saves and settings.

Known remaining reports: intermittent glide jitter and large-party slowdown
are deferred pending a reproducing recording/setup. The erroneous whole-P2
title-transition flash is repaired; exact zone-specific Kega reference flicker
remains unmatched and is explicitly partial. Of 26 original reports, 23 are
resolved, two are owner-deferred and one retains that reference caveat.
